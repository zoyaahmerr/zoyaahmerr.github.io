I am a pre-doctoral fellow at Yale, working with Shiro Kuriwaki and Joshua Kalla. My research interests are in political representation, voting rights, political behavior, and inequality, with a focus on how electoral institutions and immigration policy shape who gets to participate in political life and whose interests are represented. I graduated from the University of Pennsylvania with a B.A. in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics.
